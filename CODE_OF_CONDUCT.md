# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its
users. Harassment, insults and publishing other people's private information – including someone else's handwriting – are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

- Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue, or
- report privately to the maintainer through
  [a private report](https://github.com/migl06/penclone/security/advisories/new) (only the maintainer can see it).

The maintainer will review every report, keep it confidential, and may remove content or block participants who break these rules.
